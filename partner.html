Loomline Travel 致力于为外国游客提供高品质的中国旅行体验。我们相信，打动人心的旅程，来自对品质的共同坚持——这也是我们选择伙伴时唯一的标准。如果你与我们一样——热爱中国、对品质有要求、相信真实的在地体验能打动人心，我们很愿意与你相识。 Loomline Travel builds high-quality China travel experiences for visitors from abroad. We believe journeys that move people come from a shared commitment to quality — that is the only standard we use when choosing partners. If you love China, care about quality, and believe real local experiences can move people, we would love to meet you.

Not an open platform · 精选网络A Curated Network

不只是"供应商"More Than a Supplier

我们希望建立的是精选网络，而不是开放平台。Loomline Travel 有自己的产品体系、在地团队与服务标准——与伙伴合作，是为了让体验更深、服务更稳，而不是简单转介订单。每一次合作，都经过认真的沟通与筛选。因为品质是我们对客人的承诺，也是我们对彼此的承诺。 We aim for a curated network, not an open platform. Loomline Travel has its own product system, local team, and service standards — we partner with people to make experiences deeper and service steadier, not to pass on bookings. Every partnership goes through careful conversation and selection. Quality is our promise to guests, and our promise to each other.
