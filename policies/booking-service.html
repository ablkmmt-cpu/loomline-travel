Booking & Service Information

Last updated: August 24, 2026

We use a consultation-and-confirmation booking model. The process is as follows:

Part One: Booking Process

  1. Submit an inquiry: Tell us about your travel plans through our website form, email, or WeChat, including your dates, destinations, number of travelers, preferences, and other relevant details.
  2. Discuss your requirements: We will discuss an initial proposal with you and confirm its feasibility and the scope of services.
  3. Receive a quotation: Once we have confirmed the availability of the required services, we will send you a written quotation or itinerary proposal.
  4. Confirm payment: We will send you a secure payment link or bank transfer instructions.
  5. Booking takes effect: Once we receive the required payment, we will issue a written booking confirmation.

Please note: An inquiry, draft itinerary, or unpaid quotation does not constitute a confirmed booking.

Part Two: Service Delivery

  • Document delivery: Booking confirmations, itineraries, invoices, payment requests, and updates will be sent electronically by email, WhatsApp, or WeChat.
  • On-site services: Tours and experiences will be delivered in person in China on the confirmed date, at the confirmed time and location.
  • Customer responsibility: Please ensure that the contact details you provide are accurate and active, and notify us promptly of any changes.

Part Three: Responsibilities of Both Parties

Your responsibilities:

  • Provide accurate traveler names, dates, and service information.
  • Ensure that passports and other identity documents are valid for more than six months.
  • Tell us before booking about any dietary requirements, allergies, accessibility needs, or medical considerations.

Our responsibilities:

  • Select and coordinate all third-party suppliers, including hotels, transport providers, guides, and venues, to a reasonable standard.
  • Discuss custom itineraries with you fully before confirmation. Changes made after confirmation may require a revised quotation.

Changes: Please request any itinerary changes in writing. We will explain any additional charges before confirming the change.
