Planning process

From idea to departure

Four clear steps, with every important cost explained before you commit.

01

Share your ideas

Tell us why you are coming, when you hope to travel, who is joining, and what kind of pace or experience feels right.

Not sure where to begin? Dates, trip length, places that make you curious, and who is traveling are enough.

02

Initial guidance

We normally reply within 24 hours with practical next steps, early suggestions, and the questions that will help shape the journey.

03

Detailed design

When you are ready, an itinerary design fee of US$20–150, set according to your actual needs, covers the first itinerary, availability checks, detailed pricing, and up to two reasonable revisions.

The fee is non-refundable and is fully credited toward your trip when you confirm within 60 days. It does not include hotels, transport, tickets, guides, venues, or other actual bookings.

04

Confirm & pay

Approve the plan and pay a 40% deposit. We then begin the actual bookings. The remaining 60% is due 45 days before departure.

Supplier prepayments are always explained before payment. Trips confirmed within 45 days of departure are paid in full at confirmation.
