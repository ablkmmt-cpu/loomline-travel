Unfold your own pattern.

Chinese tie-dye turns simple cloth, string, and plant-based color into a one-of-a-kind pattern. Choose a scarf or small canvas bag, plan the design, bind it by hand, and watch the cloth change as it meets the dye. No two pieces open the same way, and no craft experience is needed.

The experience

From cloth to indigo.

The item and exact dye process vary by studio, but every session keeps the making in your hands.
  1. 01

    Meet the craft

    See how this thousand-year-old technique uses binding pressure, folds, and negative space to create traditional patterns.

    15 min
  2. 02

    Choose & design

    Select a small scarf or canvas bag and sketch the direction of your pattern before you begin.

    5 min
  3. 03

    Bind the cloth

    Use bands, clips, and folds to block the dye. Tighter binding creates sharper areas of white.

    20-25 min
  4. 04

    Dye & oxidize

    Dip the bound cloth, lift it into the air, and repeat as the color deepens through several rounds.

    20-25 min
  5. 05

    Wash & reveal

    Rinse away the extra dye and remove every knot to reveal the pattern all at once.

    10 min
  6. 06

    Dry & take it home

    Let your finished piece dry, then pack it as a usable, one-of-a-kind travel memory.

    15-20 min

What stays with you

A souvenir made by your hands.

  • A finished scarf or small canvas bag made by you
  • A practical understanding of traditional resist dyeing
  • The surprise of a pattern that cannot be repeated
  • A relaxed creative pause suitable for adults and children
