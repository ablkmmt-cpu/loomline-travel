China's most social table

Join China's mahjong table.

Mahjong is more than a game in China. It is what families, neighbors, and friends do after dinner, during holidays, and in Chengdu teahouses. You will learn the tiles, the calls, and the path to a winning hand, then play a complete guided game where calculation, luck, and personality all meet.

The experience

Learn it by playing it.

The teacher keeps the first game simple, helps every player, and explains regional variations where they matter.
  1. 01

    Meet the game

    Learn where mahjong came from, why it matters socially, and what Chinese players mean by reading a person at the table.

    15 min
  2. 02

    Learn the tiles & calls

    Recognize the 144 tiles, understand common terms, and see a simple winning hand demonstrated.

    40-50 min
  3. 03

    Practice a guided round

    Sit down, sort your hand, draw, discard, call, listen, and win with the teacher helping each move.

    30-40 min
  4. 04

    Play for points

    Move into a friendly short competition, play the agreed number of rounds, and track the score.

    20-30 min
  5. 05

    Finish with a small prize

    Celebrate the winner, take a table photo, and leave ready to join another game in China.

    10 min

What stays with you

Ready for the next table.

  • A practical grasp of the tiles and basic winning patterns
  • The confidence to follow a casual game with local friends
  • Useful calls and table etiquette in a social setting
  • A deeper sense of why mahjong matters in everyday China
