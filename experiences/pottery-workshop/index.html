Ten thousand years in your hands

Shape Chinese ceramic history.

China's ceramic story reaches from early painted pottery and Tang sancai to Song kilns and Jingdezhen porcelain. In this workshop, that long history becomes tactile. Knead the clay, center it on the wheel, raise the walls with your own hands, and add a mark or decoration before the piece meets the kiln.

The experience

Clay. Wheel. Fire. Memory.

Studio methods vary by city. If you choose firing, the finished piece normally needs two to four weeks before it can be shipped.
  1. 01

    Meet Chinese ceramics

    Follow the story from early pottery to Jingdezhen porcelain and learn why porcelain became so closely tied to China.

    15 min
  2. 02

    Knead & center

    Prepare the clay and learn to anchor it at the exact center of the spinning wheel.

    10 min
  3. 03

    Raise the form

    With the teacher guiding your hands, turn the moving clay into a cup, bowl, bottle, or another simple vessel.

    20-25 min
  4. 04

    Trim & decorate

    Refine the base, add small sculpted details or underglaze decoration, and mark the piece with a name or symbol.

    20 min
  5. 05

    Choose how it comes home

    Take the unfired piece as a keepsake, or let the studio glaze and fire it before shipping it to you at your cost.

    2-4 weeks for firing
