The experience

Listen. Choose. Unwind.

The exact session depends on your comfort and the practitioner available in your city. Nothing is presented as medical advice.
  1. 01

    A simple introduction

    Learn the everyday logic behind yin and yang, meridians, qi and blood, and why Chinese wellness often focuses on balance and flow.

    15 min
  2. 02

    Talk about how you feel

    Share your sleep, routine, stress, and areas of tension in a light wellbeing conversation rather than a medical diagnosis.

    15 min
  3. 03

    Choose your session

    The practitioner explains one or two suitable options, such as tuina, cupping, moxibustion, acupuncture, or a foot bath. You decide what feels comfortable.

    5 min
  4. 04

    Settle into the experience

    A trained practitioner guides your selected session in a calm private setting, checking your comfort throughout.

    60-90 min
  5. 05

    Close with practical context

    Notice how you feel and leave with simple lifestyle suggestions for rest and recovery, not medical instructions.

    10 min
