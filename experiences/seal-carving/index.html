Seal Carving

Turn a name into a mark of your own.
Discover

Your signature, carved in stone

Carve your own Chinese seal.

For more than two thousand years, seals have marked authority, authorship, art, and identity in China. In this workshop, your name or a meaningful Chinese character becomes a small piece of design. Draw the seal face, transfer it to stone, and carve it under close guidance before making your first red impression.

The experience

Design it. Carve it. Make your mark.

Your teacher handles the difficult translation and layout decisions with you, so first-time makers can focus on the craft.
  1. 01

    Meet the Chinese seal

    Trace the journey from imperial seals to the personal marks used by artists, collectors, and readers today.

    15 min
  2. 02

    Choose your design

    Select your Chinese name, a translated name, or a positive character and refine it with prepared references.

    15 min
  3. 03

    Transfer it to stone

    Use a brush to place the final mirror-image design onto the face of your seal stone.

    10 min
  4. 04

    Carve by hand

    Learn how to hold and guide the carving knife, then cut the lines slowly with your teacher beside you.

    35-40 min
  5. 05

    Test & refine

    Press the seal into red paste, check the clarity, and adjust shallow or uneven lines.

    15-20 min
  6. 06

    Make the final print

    Stamp the finished impression on rice paper and pack both the print and stone seal to take home.

    5 min

What stays with you

A personal mark in stone.

  • A hand-carved stone seal made during your session
  • A finished red seal impression on Chinese paper
  • The story behind seals as signatures and symbols of identity
  • Natural handmade variations that make the piece yours
