The experience

Four moments. One tea table.

The exact tea and setting vary by city, but the experience follows a clear, welcoming rhythm.
  1. 01

    Arrive & settle in

    Meet your host, take your seat, and get familiar with the teaware without needing any prior knowledge.

    10 min
  2. 02

    Meet the leaves

    Compare aroma, color, origin, and the character of the tea selected for your session.

    15 min
  3. 03

    Brew with a gaiwan

    Practice water, timing, grip, and pouring while noticing how the flavor develops over several infusions.

    30 min
  4. 04

    Tea, snacks & conversation

    Enjoy local pairings and ask the practical questions that help you feel comfortable in Chinese teahouses.

    15-35 min

What stays with you

More confidence, less ceremony.

  • A simple way to recognize and enjoy Chinese tea
  • Hands-on confidence using a gaiwan
  • Useful teahouse etiquette without rigid rules
  • Local context that connects tea with everyday life
