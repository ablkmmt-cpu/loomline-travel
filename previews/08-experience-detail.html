What you take away

Brew once. Understand China a little better.

Most visitors watch tea from the menu side. This is the other side of the table — a hands-on session where the teaware, the etiquette, and the slow pace stop being mysterious.

🫖
Brew with a gaiwan — for realWatch once, then do it yourself: water, timing, grip, and how flavor changes across infusions.
🍵
Read any teahouse like a localOrdering, refills, snacks, and the unspoken rules — comfortable in Chinese teahouses afterward.
🎁
Tea you can actually take homeLearn which teas suit you, what to buy, and how to brew them when you're back.

Four moments, one tea table

01Arrive & settle

Meet your host, take your seat, and get familiar with the teaware.

→ 无需任何基础，来了就会
02Meet the leaves

Compare aroma, color, origin, and character of the teas picked for you.

→ 找到你的茶，而不是最贵的茶
03Brew with a gaiwan

Practice water, timing, grip, and pouring across several infusions.

→ 回家也能复刻这一杯
04Tea, snacks & talk

Local pairings and the practical questions that make teahouses feel easy.

→ 明天起，茶馆不再有门槛
