Tea Culture

Not a lecture. A quiet seat at the tea table — learn to brew with a gaiwan and read the room like a local.

Add to my trip → Chat on WhatsApp
70–90 min·Chengdu · Hangzhou · Beijing·Best for first-timers & tea lovers·English host

What you take away

Brew once. Understand China a little better.

Most visitors watch tea from the menu side. This is the other side of the table — a hands-on session where the teaware, the etiquette, and the slow pace stop being mysterious.

  • Brew with a gaiwan — for real: water, timing, grip, across several infusions
  • Read any teahouse like a local: ordering, refills, snacks, unspoken rules
  • Tea you can actually take home: what suits you, what to buy, how to brew it

“Tea is how Chengdu slows down — and after this session, it's how you will too.”

— Your tea host
