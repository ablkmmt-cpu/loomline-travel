02

03

Dujiangyan

A two-thousand-year-old irrigation system still shaping Sichuan.

How long to stay

Three days cover the city and pandas; four or five leave room for Sichuan's best nearby day trips.

3days

See the pandas, drink tea in People's Park, visit Wuhou Shrine, and follow one considered Sichuan food route.

4days

Add Dujiangyan or Mount Qingcheng while keeping the city itself relaxed.

5days

Slow down further: a second food route, a teahouse afternoon, and one open day.

Practical notes

Getting there

双流/天府双机场；高铁 1.5h 到西安、3h 到重庆、7h 到上海。

Getting around

地铁覆盖主要景点，市区打车便宜；熊猫基地建议早出发。

Where to stay

春熙路/太古里周边最方便；想安静可选锦江区临河一带。

Eating

微辣起步、循序渐进；我们可带你避开游客店的本地馆子清单。
