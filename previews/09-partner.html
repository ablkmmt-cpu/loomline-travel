与 TripToChina 合作Partnerships

TripToChina 致力于为外国游客提供高品质的中国旅行体验。我们相信，打动人心的旅程，来自对品质的共同坚持——这也是我们选择伙伴时唯一的标准。如果你与我们一样——热爱中国、对品质有要求、相信真实的在地体验能打动人心，我们很愿意与你相识。TripToChina builds high-quality China travel experiences for visitors from abroad. We believe journeys that move people come from a shared commitment to quality — that is the only standard we use when choosing partners. If you love China, care about quality, and believe real local experiences can move people, we would love to meet you.

我们寻找的同行者The People We Work With

我们首先寻找认同——对品质的理解、对客人的态度、对长期合作的想法。领域是其次的。我们珍视的同行者，通常来自以下方向：We look for alignment first — how you understand quality, how you treat guests, and how you think about long-term work. The field comes second. The people we value most usually come from these directions:

壹 · 文化文化空间与手作Cultural Spaces & Crafts

茶馆、工坊、画廊、私人厨房、非遗体验馆——那些把客人真正带进中国日常的地方。Teahouses, workshops, galleries, private kitchens, heritage studios — places that bring guests into everyday China.

贰 · 服务服务与运营团队Service & Operations Teams

与我们共同保障行程顺畅与从容的伙伴，让每一次抵达都安心。Partners who help us keep journeys smooth and unhurried, so every arrival feels safe and easy.

叁 · 连接机构与社区Institutions & Communities

学校、文化机构、商会与海外社区，让旅行与更多场景和人群相连。Schools, cultural institutions, associations, and overseas communities — connecting travel to more people and moments.

Not an open platform · 精选网络A Curated Network

不只是"供应商"More Than a Supplier

我们希望建立的是精选网络，而不是开放平台。TripToChina 有自己的产品体系、在地团队与服务标准——与伙伴合作，是为了让体验更深、服务更稳，而不是简单转介订单。每一次合作，都经过认真的沟通与筛选。因为品质是我们对客人的承诺，也是我们对彼此的承诺。We aim for a curated network, not an open platform. TripToChina has its own product system, local team, and service standards — we partner with people to make experiences deeper and service steadier, not to pass on bookings. Every partnership goes through careful conversation and selection. Quality is our promise to guests, and our promise to each other.
