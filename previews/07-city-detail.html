Why this city

China's most livable city, in three flavors.

Chengdu is where first-time visitors slow down: giant pandas in the morning, teahouses and mahjong tables in the afternoon, and a food scene that runs from street stalls to hotpot banquets. It pairs naturally with Xi'an and Shanghai on a first China route.

Practical notes

Getting there

双流/天府双机场；高铁 1.5h 到西安、3h 到重庆、7h 到上海。

Getting around

地铁覆盖主要景点，市区打车便宜；熊猫基地建议早出发。

Where to stay

春熙路/太古里周边最方便；想安静可选锦江区临河一带。

Eating

微辣起步、循序渐进；我们可带你避开游客店的本地馆子清单。
